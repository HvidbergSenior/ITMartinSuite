Purpose

Game Master helps you at the table when you play role-playing games – like Dungeons & Dragons, Werewolf or a murder mystery.

Everyone can see the players: name, character, race, class, hit points and spells, so nobody forgets anything. When a player loses hit points, everyone sees it at once.

The game master can send a secret message to one player – like "You see something glinting to your right". Only that player sees it.

Notifications on your phone

Put Game Master on your home screen

Then it opens like an app with one tap. On iPhone it must be on the home screen before it can send you notifications.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)
  1. Open spilleder.itmartin.dk in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen and then Add.
  4. Open Game Master from the new icon and tap Turn on notifications.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open spilleder.itmartin.dk in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
Computer (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Open spilleder.itmartin.dk.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or the menu ⋯ → Apps → Install.
About Game Master

Game Master is for role-playing and other games where the game master has to tell one player something the others must not hear. No ads, and nothing is sold or shared.
