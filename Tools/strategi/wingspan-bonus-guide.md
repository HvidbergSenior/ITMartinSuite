## How to pick your starting bonus card
- Keep the one that matches **the birds in your starting hand** and your planned engine.
- **Share of birds that fit** is the quick guide: 30 %+ is easy, under 15 % is hard. Shares below are out of all 596 birds in the digital game (base, European, Oceania, Asia + Bird Packs).
- Cards marked "board" do not depend on single birds but on how you build your board.
- All bonus cards from every expansion are here, with the set in brackets. Search a card name (🔎) for its best birds.

## S tier – easy points in almost every game
- **Behaviorist** (European) – For each column that contains birds with 3 different power colors: – 3 pts per column. Board: a mix of brown, white and pink/teal powers in each column.
- **Breeding Manager** (Base) – Birds that have at least 4 eggs laid on them – 1 pt per bird. Board: birds that hold 4+ eggs and egg-laying powers.
- **Ecologist** (Base) – Birds in your habitat with the fewest birds. – 2 pts per bird. Board: a balanced board – keep your smallest habitat growing.
- **Oologist** (Base) – Birds that have at least 1 egg laid on them – 7 to 8 birds: 3 pts; 9+ birds: 6 pts. Board: many cheap birds with at least 1 egg each.
- **Visionary Leader** (Base) – Bird cards in hand at end of game – 5 to 7 birds: 4 pts; 8+ birds: 7 pts. Board: card-draw birds; keep 5-8 cards in hand at the end.

- **Platform Builder** (Base) – Birds with platform nests – 4 to 5 birds: 4 pts; 6+ birds: 7 pts. 31 % of birds fit.

- **Diet Specialist** (European) – Birds with a food cost of 3 food – 2 to 3 birds: 3 pts; 4+ birds: 6 pts. 28 % of birds fit.

- **Citizen Scientist** (European) – Birds with tucked cards – 4 to 6 birds: 3 pts; 7+ birds: 6 pts. Board: tuck birds (flocking) spread over many birds.

- **Winter Feeder** (Asia) – Food remaining in your supply at game end – 4 to 6 food: 4 pts; 7+ food: 7 pts. Board: food engines; stop spending food in the last turns.

## Raven side note
- Ravens empty eggs: if you face a raven player, the egg bonus cards (Oologist, Breeding Manager, Avian Theriogenologist) are weaker for you, because a raven player can empty your eggs.
