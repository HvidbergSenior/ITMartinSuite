CharacterKolibri Kommunikere

Purpose

Character helps you make a figure for a role-playing game – step by step: people, class, ability scores, hit points, gear, spells, picture and story.

Your game master can open the same figure and help you live – at the table or from another place. You can write to each other on every step.

When the figure is ready, your game master brings it into Game Master with a short code. Everything is saved as you type.

Codes and sharing
Put Character on your home screen

Then it opens like an app with one tap.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)
  1. Open character.itmartin.dk in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen and then Add.
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open character.itmartin.dk in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
Computer (Chrome or Edge)
  1. Open character.itmartin.dk.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or the menu ⋯ → Apps → Install.
